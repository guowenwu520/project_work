ChangeBlindnessRoom smooth camera route v6
===========================================

Purpose
-------
Fix the visible camera rotation jump at the boundary between route movement
and the phase that turns back toward the tabletop.

What changed
------------
1. Camera position and orientation now come from one planned smooth route.
2. The actual route derivative defines the walking rotation at every point.
3. The final route rotation is reused exactly as the first rotation of the
   return-to-table phase.
4. SmootherStep is used on both sides of every phase boundary, so position,
   rotation and their velocities reach the same boundary state.
5. Natural eye motion uses a continuous walking weight instead of switching
   abruptly between walking/non-walking modes.
6. Sequence completion no longer overwrites the already evaluated final pose.
7. No extra transition stage or extra seconds were added.
8. Both route variants stay inside the original camera orbit; the destination
   and all detours move slightly toward the table to avoid walls/windows.
9. The last 18% of the walking route begins the same signed turn that continues
   in returnToTable. About 15% of the tabletop turn is completed before the
   phase boundary, removing the visible direction change without extra time.
10. The initial stationary turn is limited to 30 degrees. The first 32% of
    walking then blends continuously from that gentle turn into the actual
    route tangent, removing the initial movement-boundary view jump.
11. Route 2 now uses one wide, shallow inward sweep for every final angle.
    The previous 1-4 repeated bends were removed, and its extra inset is
    limited to 0.05-0.10m to prevent repeated left/right camera swings.

Preserved behavior
------------------
- Random timing: observation 2-10 seconds; movement 2-7 seconds.
- Total duration range: 10-41 seconds (five existing stages only).
- Final view angles: 45, 90, 135 and 180 degrees.
- Two route variants for every final view angle.
- Extreme profiles:
  longest route + fastest timing;
  shortest route + slowest timing.
- Scene-only annotation/video generation. No QA files are generated here.

Apply
-----
Extract this archive at the project root and allow it to replace the matching
files under Assets/Scripts.

Rebuild
-------
UNITY_BIN=/data4/UnitySceneGeneration/Unity/Hub/Editor/2022.3.58f1c1/Editor/Unity \
./Tools/build_linux.sh

Run existing route tests
------------------------
TEST_FEATURE=camera_route ./Tools/test_all_cases.sh

The command-line parser accepts these equivalent route option spellings:
--camera-angle / --camera-final-angle / --camera-end-angle / --final-view-angle
--camera-route-variant / --route-variant / --camera-route
--camera-route-profile / --route-profile
